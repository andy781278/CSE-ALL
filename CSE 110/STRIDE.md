Threat modeling
This is all possible threats

Spoofing identity
- Pretending to be someone else to get clearance
Tampering with data
- Changing data so its advantageous to the attacker
Repudiation
- Fake reports, saying there's a security breach / hack done to you when you're just trying to scam
Information disclosure
- Extracting information to someone who's not supposed to know
Denial of service
- Attacks that prevents the service from working properly
- like d-dos, sending a huge amount of requests that crash the server
Elevation of privilege
- Someone pretending to be of a higher level privilege trying to do stuff they're not supposed to.
- students editing their own grades
