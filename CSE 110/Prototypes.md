
| Variable  | Design Heuristic                                                                                                                                                                      |
| --------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Testing   | Construct a clear testing objective, focus on a question                                                                                                                              |
| Timing    | Early prototyping is the most critical, late prototyping is not worth                                                                                                                 |
| Ideation  | Prototypes leads to functional ideas                                                                                                                                                  |
| Fixation  | Fast prototyping reduces fixation                                                                                                                                                     |
| Feedback  | Feedback may induce corrections but also increase fixation                                                                                                                            |
| Usability | End-user testing may enhance performance assessment accuracy                                                                                                                          |
| Fidelity  | Higher fidelity representations lead to accurate interpretations of the design. More Fidelity is more interactivity level. Managers expect high fidelity, so show peers low fidelity. |
