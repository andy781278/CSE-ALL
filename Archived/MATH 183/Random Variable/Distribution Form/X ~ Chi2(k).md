# $X \textasciitilde \chi^2(k)$

if $Z_1,Z_2,..,Z_k$ are independent normal standard random variables | $Z_i \sim N(0,1)$ then the sum of their squares are X:
$$X=Z_1^2+Z_2^2+...+Z_k^2$$
$$PDF: F_X(x)=\frac{1}{2^{k/2}\Gamma(k/2)}x^{k/2-1}e^{-x/2}$$
$$supp(X)=R_+$$
$$E(X)=k$$
$$Var(X)=2k$$
Let $X \textasciitilde \chi^2(d)$, $Y \textasciitilde \chi^2(k)$, and $X \perp Y$, then
$$X+Y \textasciitilde \chi^2(d+k)$$
