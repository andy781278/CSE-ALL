Bernoulli Distribution

# Discrete
When a random variable 𝑋 can only take **two** possible outcomes, it is referred to as a Bernoulli random variable.

$$ 
X=\begin{cases}
1 & \text{with probability p} \\
0 & \text{with probability 1-p}
\end{cases}
$$
$$p_X(x)=p^x(1-p)^{1-x}$$
$$E(X)=p$$
$$E(X^2)=p$$
$$Var(X)=p-p^2$$
