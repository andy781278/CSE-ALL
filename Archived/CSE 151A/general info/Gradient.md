$$\nabla f(x)=\Sigma = \begin{pmatrix} \frac{\partial f}{\partial x_1}(x) \\  \frac{\partial f}{\partial x_2}(x) \\ ... \\ \frac{\partial f}{\partial x_d}(x) \end{pmatrix} \in \mathbb{R}^d$$
A vector of all the partial derivatives, a representation of the derivative of a multivariable function.


> [!note] Key fact
> $\nabla f(x)$ points in the direction of the steepest ascent of f at x, so $-\nabla f(x)$ points in the greatest descent
