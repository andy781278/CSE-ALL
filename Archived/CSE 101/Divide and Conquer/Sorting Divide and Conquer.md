Sorting is as if you are finding a unique permutation of the list that matches what you want. So there must be n! unique permutations. This is like traversing down a tree where you traverse by comparing two elements, and go left for <=, and right for >, then finding the answer on a leaf.

A sorting algorithm must have minimum $\Omega(n!) = \Omega(nlogn)$
