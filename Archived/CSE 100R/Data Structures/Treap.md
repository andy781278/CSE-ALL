##### Find
Time (average): $O(log(n))$
Time (worst case): $O(n)$
##### Insert
Time (average): $O(log(n))$
Time (worst case): $O(n)$
##### Remove
Time (average): $O(log(n))$
Time (worst case): $O(n)$

Same as a BST, but to maintain the heap priority property, we can perform AVL rotations after an algorithm

##### Random Search Tree
A Treap that gives a random priority to every key
