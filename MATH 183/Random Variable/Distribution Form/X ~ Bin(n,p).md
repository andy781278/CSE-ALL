number of successes in 𝑛 𝑖𝑖𝑑 trials from a Ber(𝑝) random variable

$$X=\#\{Z_i=1:1<=i<=n\}=\sum_{i=1}^n Z_i$$

$$P(X=k)=\binom{n}{k}p^k(1-p)^{n-k}$$
$$E(X)=np$$
$$Var(X)=np(1-p)$$
if $X \textasciitilde Bin(n,p)$ and $H \textasciitilde Bin(m,p)$ and $X \perp Y$, then 
$$X+Y=Bin(n+m,p)$$
