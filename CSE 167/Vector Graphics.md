In this class, an image (digital) is a function $I(x,y)$ of colors with $x,y \in \mathbb{R}$

we sample the image at certain intervals to create a representation called a **raster image**
- Screens only take raster images, but in mathematics, images don't have to be discrete, then can be continuous, i.e. no pixels or smallest degree of representations
- Turning an image into raster is called **rendering**
- Let's call the un-rastered images **vector graphics**
	- more convenient storage wise, since it's less information
